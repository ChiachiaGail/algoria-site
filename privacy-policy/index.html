9. Sailfleet

Sailfleet is an online game with its own game server. In addition to section 8, the following applies to Sailfleet.

9.1 Sign-in and account data

  • Device identifier: a random identifier created by the app on first launch and stored on your device, used to sign you in as a guest. On Android we also read the device identifier the system provides to this app (Android ID) and keep only a cryptographic hash of it on our server, so we can recognize the same device after you reinstall and restore your guest account. It is not the advertising ID, is not used for ads, and is never linked to the advertising ID.
  • Google Play Games (optional): if you choose to link, we use Google Play Games Services (Recall) to associate your game account with your Play Games profile. We store only a random link token we generate. We do not receive or store your email address, name or Google account ID.
  • Transfer code (optional): if you create a transfer code, we store only a keyed hash of it. The code is shown to you once and works once.
  • Game data: progress, ships, crew, resources, captain name, arena rating and fleet. Captain name, progress, arena rating and fleet are shown on leaderboards and to other players in the arena.
  • Purchases: product, price, store order ID and the game account it belongs to, to deliver purchases, prevent fraud and for accounting. An opaque account tag is attached to the purchase at Google Play so it is credited to the right account. We never receive your card or payment details.
  • IP address: used transiently to limit request rates and prevent abuse (for example, transfer-code attempts). Not stored with your game profile; server logs are kept for up to 30 days.

9.2 Ads

  • Sailfleet only uses rewarded video ads: a video plays only when you tap "Watch" to claim a bonus. There are no interstitial or banner ads.
  • Ads are served by Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID and approximate location to select and measure ads. Where consent is required (such as the EEA and UK), we ask for your choice first through Google's consent message.

9.3 Where the data is stored

Sailfleet's game servers are located in Hong Kong SAR.

9.4 Deleting your Sailfleet account

  • In the game: Settings → Account → Delete account. On the web: sf.algoriagame.com/delete with your transfer code. No code? Use our support form or email agrhorra@gmail.com with your Player ID (shown in Settings). Please do not email your transfer code.
  • Deleted immediately: captain name, leaderboard and arena entries, linked sign-ins (device, Play Games link, transfer code) and feedback you sent; the account disappears from the game right away.
  • 14-day grace period: a copy of your game progress is kept for 14 days so that an accidental deletion can be undone by contacting support (include your current Player ID). After 14 days it is permanently deleted.
  • Deleted within 30 days: analytics data linked to your account. Server backups roll over within 30 days; security logs are kept for up to 30 days.
  • Kept: purchase records (product, amount, order ID) for tax and accounting, without your name or game data.
  • If a Sailfleet account is inactive for 24 months in a row, we may delete or anonymize it.
