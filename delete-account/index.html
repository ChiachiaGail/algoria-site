Delete account & data

How to delete your game account and the data we hold about you.

Games without an account

Most Algoria games (for example Pengdoku, Tango Pals, Shape Puzzle Master and 5-in-1 Solitaire) do not create an account. Your progress is stored only on your phone.

  • To delete your game data: uninstall the game, or go to your phone's Settings → Apps → the game → Storage → Clear data.
  • To delete data held by us or our partners (analytics, crash reports, support messages): submit a request using the steps below.

Sailfleet

  • In the game: Settings → Account → Delete account.
  • On the web: go to sf.algoriagame.com/delete and enter your transfer code.
  • No transfer code? Submit a request below and include your Player ID (shown in Settings). Never send us your transfer code.
  • Sailfleet keeps a copy of your progress for 14 days so an accidental deletion can be undone; after that it is permanently deleted. Full details are in the privacy policy.

Submit a deletion request

  1. Open our customer service form.
  2. Choose the game and write "Delete my account and data".
  3. Include your Player ID if the game shows one, and the email address we should reply to.

You can also email agrhorra@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data".

What happens next

  • We may ask you to confirm that the account is yours.
  • We confirm your request within 7 days and complete the deletion within 30 days.
  • Deleted: your game account, progress, items, player name, leaderboard entries, and analytics data linked to your account or device.
  • Kept: purchase records required by tax and accounting law (kept only as long as the law requires), and anonymous statistics that can no longer identify you.
  • Deletion cannot be undone. Items bought in a deleted account cannot be restored or refunded.

Refunds for purchases are handled by Google Play or the App Store. Deleting your account does not cancel or refund a purchase.
