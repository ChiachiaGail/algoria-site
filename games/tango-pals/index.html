Tango Pals: Cute Logic Puzzle

Solve cozy logic puzzles to grow, harvest and decorate your own clay garden!

Get it onGoogle Play

Tango Pals is a cozy logic puzzle where every board you solve grows your garden. Plant a seed with each win, watch carrots, tomatoes and pumpkins ripen, then harvest them for stars to open new plots, decorate with fences, flowers and a pond, and dress up the board with new clay pals.

From level 25, Trio boards join the mix: three pals instead of two, with exactly two of each in every row and column. It's a fresh twist on classic binary puzzles that you won't find anywhere else. Want a taste right away? Tap "Try a Trio board" in your garden.

🌱 GROW YOUR GARDEN
• Each solved puzzle plants a seed. Two more wins and it's ripe.
• Tap ripe veggies to harvest them for stars and fill your basket.
• Eight crops to unlock, from carrots and radishes to corn and peas.
• Spend stars to open new plots, up to 12, and grow more veggies at once.
• Decorate your way: picket fences, flower bushes, sunflowers, mushroom lamps, a pond, a bench, a beehive and more. Small pieces can be bought again and again, so you can line the garden with fences or fill it with flowers.
• Trade your harvest at the Farm Stand: village folk post new veggie orders every day.

🏆 GARDEN FAIR
• A new 24-hour fair every day against the village gardens of Bunny Village. Solve puzzles and harvest veggies to climb the board.
• Finish in the top spots for bonus stars and hints. The fair is a single-player event and works offline.

🐰 HOW TO PLAY
• Tap a square to cycle through the pals: bunny, carrot, and on trio boards, lettuce.
• Every row and column holds the same number of each pal.
• On classic boards, never put three of the same side by side.
• "=" between two squares means they match. "×" means they're different.
• A full row or column that breaks a rule turns red. Fix it in time or a heart wilts.

🥕 WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT
• Over 2,600 puzzles, every one verified to have exactly one answer. No guessing, ever.
• Hints that teach: see the next deduction and the reason behind it.
• Stuck? Undo, Lucky 3 or Reveal row help you through tricky spots.
• Three stars for finishing fast without losing a heart.
• A new Daily Puzzle every day from level 10, with special trio boards on weekends.
• A daily gift waiting in your garden, with bigger rewards on day 3 and day 7.
• Dress up the board with Bear & Honey, Mouse & Cheese or Fox & Blueberry.
• Squishy hand-made clay art, gentle music and an easy one-minute tutorial.
• Puzzles grow a little harder as you go, with bigger boards along the way.
• Play offline, anywhere.

Most puzzles take just a few minutes, so your garden grows a little every coffee break. Tango Pals is free and supported by optional ads.

Grab a watering can and give your brain a little workout!
