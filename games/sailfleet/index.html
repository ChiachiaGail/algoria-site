Sailfleet: Pirate Ship Battle

Raise legendary ships, line up your fleet and rule the seas in 3-lane battles!

Get it onGoogle Play

Pirates have closed the sea lanes. You are the captain who opens them again.

Raise sunken ships from the deep, build a fleet of three, and send them into fast three-lane sea battles. Every lane you clear sends cargo ships home, and your port keeps earning even while you are away.

Build your fleet

Over 40 hand-painted ships, from small coastal sloops to legendary galleons, each with its own skill. Put each ship on the lane that suits it.

Battles where the wind matters

Square, lateen and mixed rigs each sail best in a different wind. Tap crew skills at the right moment and focus fire on the most dangerous enemy.

A crew with character

Penguins, otters, parrots, cats, seals and capybaras. Every crew member has a role and a skill.

Play your way

Your port earns while you're offline. Ads are optional: you only watch a video when you choose to, for a bonus. Sailfleet is free to play; some items can be bought with real money.
