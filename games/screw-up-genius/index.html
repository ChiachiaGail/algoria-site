Screw Up Genius

Solve creative screw puzzles and master your tools!

Twist, fix, and screw your way through challenging puzzles! Use various tools to tighten screws, unlock tricky levels, and become a fixing genius.

Screw Up Genius is an addictive puzzle game where your tool skills are put to the test! Rotate screws, hammer nails, and tighten bolts in a series of fun and engaging levels. Features:

100+ levels of challenging screw puzzles
Use different tools: hammers, drills, screwdrivers, and more
Simple yet addictive gameplay
Colorful graphics with smooth animations
Unlock new tools and upgrades as you advance
No time limits – play at your own pace
