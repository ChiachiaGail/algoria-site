Petal Pots: Pour & Bloom

Drag pots to the petal soil, fill them up and watch them bloom before sunset!

Get it onGoogle Play

Garden beds heaped with coloured petal soil, empty flower pots, and one satisfying pour!

Drag a pot against a bed and soil of its colour streams in. Slide it right under the bed and the whole column pours down. A full pot bursts into bloom and hops onto your garden cart. Empty every bed before the sun sets, and watch the cart roll off with a bouquet.

HOW TO PLAY
• Drag a pot to a bed: soil of the pot's colour pours in
• Soil falls as it drains, so new colours come to the edge. Plan which pot goes next
• A full pot blooms and flies to the cart. Fill the cart to win
• Beat the sunset timer. Frozen pots, rails, gates and pipes make later gardens trickier

FEATURES
• 300+ levels and more on the way, from gentle warm-ups to Very Hard gardens
• New garden tricks every few levels: frost, rails, twin pots, gates, pipes, glass, boulders and more
• Boosters when you are stuck: Sundial, Trowel, Watering Can, Bee Swarm and Early Bird
• Grow your Garden: every win drops a seed, and 10 seeds plant a new flower species. 30 species to collect
• Daily bonus calendar and garden chests
• Play offline, anytime
• 10 languages

Petal Pots is free to play with optional in-app purchases and ads.
