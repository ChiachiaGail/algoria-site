Arrow Garden: Tap Away Puzzle

Tap away arrows to reveal hidden garden pictures. Calm puzzles, no timer.

Get it onGoogle Play

Every board hides a garden picture. Tap the arrows away and watch it bloom!

Arrow Garden is a calm puzzle. There is no timer, so think at your own pace. Each arrow bends along its own path and slides out when its way is clear. Clear the whole board to reveal a cute pixel picture: flowers, fruit, animals and more.

HOW TO PLAY
• Tap an arrow. If its path is free, it slides off the board.
• If something is in the way, the arrow turns red and you lose a heart. You have 3 hearts.
• Clear every arrow to reveal the hidden picture.

WHAT YOU'LL LOVE
• 152 pictures to collect in your Garden Album
• Endless levels, from gentle to Super Hard
• A new Daily Garden puzzle every day, with a calendar and a streak
• Hints, an Eraser and a Grid view when you get stuck
• Pinch to zoom and drag to look around big boards
• A warm wooden garden look with soft sounds

Relax, tap, and grow your garden.
