Link Color Lines

Connect and Fill. Conquer the Grid!

Welcome to Line Color Lines – the ultimate puzzle game where you connect colorful blocks and fill the grid to solve each level! With a variety of levels ranging from easy to impossible, there's something for every skill level.

Game Rules:
1. Connect Blocks: Draw lines to connect blocks of the same color.
2. Fill the Grid: Ensure every empty space in the grid is filled to complete the level.

Features:
1 Unlock Levels: Progress through the game to unlock higher levels and bundles.
2. Earn Rewards: Collect stars, bonuses, and gifts as you play.
3. Variety of Challenges: With both square grids and irregular shapes, there's always a new challenge to enjoy.
