Parking Maze

Navigate and Park with Puzzle Panache！

Get it onGoogle PlayDownload on theApp Store

Ready to take on the dual challenge of parking and maze-solving?

Parking Maze is an addictive puzzle game for all ages that blends the fun of parking with the brain-teasing twists of a maze, offering a unique gaming experience!

Game Features:

**A Multitude of Mazes Await Your Challenge!
Four difficulty levels: Easy, Medium, Hard, and Expert, with hundreds of carefully crafted levels to cater to players of all skill sets.

**Exquisite Graphics, Visually Delightful!
Featuring pixel art that creates a refreshing and vibrant gaming atmosphere, each parking lot scene is unique, providing an immersive gaming experience.

**Simple Controls, Easy to Master!
Simply swipe the screen to control the vehicle's movement, effortlessly completing parking maneuvers.

**Star Ratings, Challenge Yourself!
Earn star ratings based on your moves in each level; complete the level with the fewest moves possible to achieve the coveted three-star rating!

**Customizable Settings, Tailor Your Experience!
Freely toggle music and sound effects, adjust volume levels, and create your perfect gaming ambiance in the settings menu.

**Hints and Undo, Assisting Your Progress!
Utilize the hint feature when stuck to gain clues for solving puzzles; the undo function allows you to retrace your steps, effortlessly overcoming challenges.

Gameplay:

1,Select Level: Choose a level from various difficulty levels to embark on your parking adventure.
2,Maneuver Your Vehicle: Swipe the screen to control the vehicle's movement, finding the optimal path to park the designated car in the specified spot.
3,Unlock New Levels: Successfully completing a level unlocks the next one, allowing you to tackle more challenging parking mazes.

Whether you're a parking novice or a seasoned driver,
Parking Maze will provide you with endless fun and challenges!

Download now and experience the thrilling fusion of parking and puzzle-solving!
