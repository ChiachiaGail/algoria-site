Hero of Kalanda

Hold the wall! Pick skills, lead 20 heroes and build your dream team.

Get it onGoogle Play

The monsters of Kalanda are coming in waves. Only your hero and the wall stand in their way.

⚔️ EVERY RUN IS DIFFERENT
Your hero fires automatically — you make the choices that matter. Level up during the battle and pick a skill upgrade each time. Mix fire, ice, wind, light, dark and physical power, chain your favourite combos and find the build that breaks the wave.

🛡️ 20 HEROES, 20 SIGNATURE SKILLS
Recruit 20 heroes across six elements, each with a skill of their own: Sacred Slash, Howling Blizzard, Fox-Fire Whirlwind, Repair Drone and more. Take four into battle, level them up, raise their stars and unlock stronger skill effects.

🗺️ 100 STAGES OF RISING DANGER
Fight through 100 stages, each tougher than the last, with mighty bosses guarding the way. Plan your team, upgrade your gear and push deeper into Kalanda.

💎 GEAR UP YOUR WAY
Six equipment slots, six quality tiers, gems to socket and stats to reroll. Every upgrade changes how your squad fights.

🎁 REWARDS EVERY DAY
Daily sign-in rewards, daily and 7-day quests, free daily hero draws and a treasure chest that refills through the day.

🌐 5 LANGUAGES
Play in English, 日本語, 한국어, 繁體中文 or 简体中文.

Short sessions, real choices, steady progress. Defend the wall — become the hero of Kalanda!
