Pengdoku: Penguin Logic Puzzle

Cute star battle logic puzzle. One penguin per color, row & column. Play daily!

Get it onGoogle Play

Pengdoku is a cozy logic puzzle game with one simple rule: place exactly one penguin in every color, every row and every column, and never let two penguins touch, not even at the corners.

Fans of Queens, Star Battle and sudoku will feel at home in seconds. New to logic puzzles? The built-in tutorial teaches you everything in under a minute.

🐧 HOW TO PLAY
• Tap a square to mark it with an X. Swipe to mark a whole line at once.
• Double-tap to place a penguin.
• Every color, row and column gets exactly one penguin.
• Penguins can't touch, diagonals included.
• You have three fish. A wrong penguin costs one. Lose all three and you can watch a short ad to revive.

✨ WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT
• Hundreds of levels, from quick 4×4 warm-ups to brain-bending 10×10 boards.
• Every puzzle has exactly one solution and can be solved by pure logic. No guessing.
• Smart hints that teach the next deduction instead of giving away the answer.
• Reveal a penguin when you're stuck, or try Lucky X for a gentle nudge.
• A new Daily Challenge every day, the same board for everyone. Beat the clock for bonus stars.
• Check in 7 days in a row for star rewards.
• Spend stars in the Star Shop to play as a cat, dog, capybara, cow or horse.
• Earn up to three stars per level with zero mistakes.
• Works offline. Play on the train, in bed, anywhere.
• Soft pastel colors, gentle animations and one very fluffy penguin.

🧠 A BRAIN GAME THAT RESPECTS YOUR TIME
Levels take two to ten minutes. There's no energy system, no timers, no forced waits. Pengdoku is free, supported by optional ads.

Perfect for fans of logic puzzles, sudoku, nonogram, queens game, star battle, brain teasers and cute animal games.

Waddle in and give your brain a little workout!
