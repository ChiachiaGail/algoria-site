Donut Knots: Ring Puzzle

Twist glazed donuts to free the pretzel sticks and fill the bakery box!

Get it onGoogle Play

A tangle of glazed donuts, pretzel sticks and one satisfying twist!

Every donut has a little bite missing. Turn a donut until its bite slides past a pretzel stick, and the stick slips free. A donut that nothing holds any more hops into the bakery box. Clear the whole tray to win, and watch the cascades fill the box in a rush.

HOW TO PLAY
• Drag a donut to turn it around its centre
• A stick comes out when the bite of the donut it sits in passes over it
• A donut held by nothing hops into the box, taking its own sticks with it
• Plan the order: a freed stick can get caught again

FEATURES
• 1,000 levels, from gentle warm-ups to Very Hard knots
• Churros, cookie rings, jam fillings and sugar locks keep it fresh
• Oven Timer on Hard levels for an extra thrill
• Helpful boosters: Undo, Sprinkle Rocket and Rolling Pin
• Daily bonus calendar and treat chests
• Play offline, anytime
• 10 languages

Donut Knots is free to play with optional in-app purchases and ads.
