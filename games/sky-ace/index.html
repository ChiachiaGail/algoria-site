Sky Ace

Fly, Collect, Rank Up, from Cadet to Sky Marshal!

Get it onGoogle PlayDownload on theApp Store

Swoop into Sky Ace's adventure for sky-high thrills! Zip through a world of fluffy clouds in your nifty plane, dodging foes and snagging coins. It's all about quick moves and cool flights.
Rack up the wins and the gold in every daring mission!
Features:
• Fly & Dodge: Your skills determine your fate as you maneuver to avoid collisions in the vast blue playground.
• Collect & Earn: Snatch coins mid-air to set earnings records with each successful flight.
• Rank & Rise: Start as a Cadet and rise through the ranks to become a legendary Sky
Marshal with each triumph.
• Refuel & Resume: Short on fuel? Head to the store to refuel using your hard-earned coins or watch a quick ad to get back to your skyward journey.
• Track Your Stats: Visit your cockpit to check stats and monitor your progress.
• Badge of Honor: Earn badges for your victories and display them proudly in your gallery.
Sky Ace is more than just a game; it's a quick escape to a world above, perfect for those moments when you need a break. Whether you're looking to pass the time or become the ultimate sky ace, your adventure awaits. Ready for takeoff?
