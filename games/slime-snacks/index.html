Slime Snacks: Candy Puzzle

Tap hungry slimes to gobble candies of their colour and reveal cute pictures!

Get it onGoogle Play

Hungry slimes, sweet candy pictures, and one simple tap!

Tap a colourful slime and watch it hop onto the candy track around the picture. It gobbles every candy of its own colour it can see, until its tummy is full. Plan your order, keep the jars free, and clear every candy to reveal the picture.

HOW TO PLAY
• Tap the slime at the front of a line to send it riding around the picture
• It eats the first candy it sees in each row and column, if the colour matches
• Still hungry after a lap? It waits in a jar. Don't fill every jar!
• Two slimes of the same colour in jars squish together into one bigger slime

FEATURES
• 1,000 levels with nearly 400 cute pictures
• Mystery slimes, buddy slimes and extra-hard candies keep it fresh
• Helpful boosters: Extra Jar, Spoon, Stir, Candy Bomb and Rainbow Slime
• Collect every finished picture in your album
• Daily bonus calendar
• Play offline, anytime
• 10 languages

Slime Snacks is free to play with optional in-app purchases and ads.
